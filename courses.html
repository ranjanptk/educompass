Post-SEE Academic Streams & CTEVT Diplomas

Compare +2 Science, Management, Humanities, Law, and 3-Year CTEVT Technical Diplomas in Civil, IT, and Pharmacy.

NEB +2 Stream

+2 Science Stream

Unlocks Engineering (IOE), Medical (MBBS), IT (BSc CSIT), and Biotechnology pathways.

• Core Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology / Computer Science

• Min. GPA: 2.8 to 3.6 depending on college

NEB +2 Stream

+2 Management Stream

Prepares you for BBA, Hotel Management (BHM), CA, CS, and Banking careers.

• Core Subjects: Accountancy, Economics, Business Studies, Computer Science

• Min. GPA: 1.6 to 2.4

3-Year Diploma

CTEVT Diploma in Engineering / Pharmacy

Practical hands-on technical education (Civil Overseas, Electrical, Computer, Lab Technician) enabling immediate employment or Bachelor entry.

• Duration: 3 Years

• Min. Eligibility: Minimum C Grade in Math and Science in SEE
