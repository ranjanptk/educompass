⚖️ 5-Year Integrated BALLB Entrance Overview

Admission to 5-Year BALLB in Nepal (TU Nepal Law College, Kathmandu School of Law, Chakrabarti) requires passing the competitive University Law Entrance Exam comprising Legal Aptitude, English, and Essay Writing.

30 Marks Legal Aptitude & Constitutional Law
30 Marks General Knowledge & Current Affairs
40 Marks English & Subjective Essay Writing
